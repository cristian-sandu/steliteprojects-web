# Public contact email forwarding

contact@steliteprojects.com forwards to steliteprojects@gmail.com using free Cloudflare Email Routing.

Activated on 5 October 2026 after Cloudflare confirmed the destination address was verified. The enabled exact-address routing rule is 2ff1fe3a724d4bfc8ca39a3bf7f2abcf. Apex mail DNS records were installed through Cloudflare's routing DNS endpoint; no existing apex MX provider was replaced, and the forms subdomain was retained.

The website public email uses contact@steliteprojects.com. Catch-all routing is not enabled. The contact form also delivers only to steliteprojects@gmail.com. Its fixed recipient, restricted email binding, 50-enquiries-per-day global cap and visitor limits remain enforced. No paid plan was enabled.
